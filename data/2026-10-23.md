Method: 概念分析与形式化论证：放宽“被估计量必须是参数”的假设，建立更一般的估计框架；提炼文献中刻画估计的三条假设（真值唯一、先于测量存在、独立于测量存在），并将其与另外两条假设——量子态完备性（无隐变量）和探针系统与被测系统之间存在纠缠——进行联合相容性分析，证明no-go定理。

Result: 不可能性定理：上述三条“真值”假设与量子态完备性及探针—被测系统纠缠不能同时成立。由于该纠缠是标准量子理论的普遍预言，故若不接受隐变量，也不引入在某一尺度或区域破坏纠缠的新物理机制，则无法维持估计的实在论解释。

Conclusion: 量子参数估计的“真实值”实在论解释在标准量子理论内部不可实现；要保留它必须付出代价——接受隐变量或修改标准量子理论（引入破坏纠缠的新机制）。更一般的估计框架为处理被估计量为量子变量的情形提供了基础。

Abstract: We examine the conditions under which quantum parameter estimation is compatible with the concept of a ``true'' quantity value: a unique value of the estimated quantity that exists prior to and independently of measurement. While quantum parameter estimation typically assumes that the quantity to be estimated is a parameter, we argue that this assumption can be lifted, opening the way to a more general formulation of estimation that may apply even in situations where the quantity to be estimated is no longer a parameter, but instead a quantum variable susceptible to quantum fluctuations. We identify three assumptions that characterize the way estimation is often presented in the quantum metrology literature, namely, as a procedure for inferring an unknown ``true value", which is assumed to be unique and to exist prior to and independently of measurement. Using our more general framework, we prove a no-go theorem showing that the preceding assumptions are jointly incompatible with two further assumptions: the completeness of the quantum state (no `hidden variables'), and the presence of entanglement between the probe systems and the system under measurement. We note that the presence of such entanglement is a generic prediction of standard quantum theory. Consequently, if one is not willing to accept hidden variables, nor willing to modify standard quantum theory by introducing a new physical mechanism that destroys entanglement at some scale or regime, then a realist account of estimation is ultimately impossible.
