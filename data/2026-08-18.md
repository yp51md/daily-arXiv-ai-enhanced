Method: 作者将ν<1填充态视为由ν=1处相互作用生成的斯格明子晶格陈绝缘体中移除层赝自旋斯格明子形成的、电荷为+e的斯格明子空穴金属。这些空穴具有内在的多带自由度。基于此前提出的理想极限，作者首先建立有效场论表明斯格明子空穴自身可以分数化；随后聚焦ν=2/3，构造了显式的变分尝试波函数，并通过数值证据和一般性论证表明该过程在能量上有利。

Result: 作者提供了数值证据和一般性论证，证明了斯格明子分数化过程在能量上是有利的，从而建立了从斯格明子空穴分数化到电荷分数化的完整物理图景，实现了ν=2/3处的斯格明子分数陈绝缘体。

Conclusion: 该工作确立了实现FQAH的一种现实路径，该路径不依赖部分填充的陈带，而是由集体赝自旋织构（斯格明子）的分数化产生，为理解斜方石墨烯莫尔超晶格中的FQAH效应提供了全新的微观视角。

Abstract: We propose an unconventional microscopic origin for the fractional quantum anomalous Hall (FQAH) effect in rhombohedral graphene moiré superlattices: skyrmion fractionalization. We view the state at filling $ν<1$ as a metal of skyrmion vacancies, charge $+e$ objects formed by removing layer-pseudospin skyrmions from the interaction-generated skyrmion lattice Chern insulator at $ν=1$. These vacancies are intrinsically multiband degrees of freedom, absent in single Chern band-projected studies. Building on a recently proposed ideal limit, we first develop an effective field theory showing that skyrmion vacancies can themselves fractionalize, thereby inducing charge fractionalization. Focusing on $ν=\frac{2}{3}$, we then construct explicit variational trial wavefunctions for the resulting skyrmion fractional Chern insulator and provide numerical evidence, together with general arguments, showing that this process is energetically favored. Our results establish a realistic route to the FQAH that does not rely on a partially filled Chern band, but instead arises from fractionalization of collective pseudospin textures.
