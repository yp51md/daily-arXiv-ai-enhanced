### [1] [Quantum interaction can superactivate cheating under parallel repetition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31223)
*Archishna Bhattacharyya,Laura Mančinska,Yuming Zhao*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究量子通信下的多证明者博弈与多轮协议，发现并行重复对量子通信具有反直觉的负面效果：存在单实例局部值小于1、但在n重并行重复下对任意n≥2均为1的量子博弈与QMIP协议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典情况下并行重复能将双证明者博弈的可靠性误差压至任意接近零，即使采用量子策略也成立。作者试图探究当验证者与证明者之间的通信本身为量子时，并行重复是否仍具有这一降噪性质。

Method: 构造一回合量子博弈与两回合QMIP协议，利用交换态中的纠缠可在多个副本间联合利用的特性；建立量子博弈/交互协议与量子信道零误差容量的对应关系（含纠缠辅助与否）；并使用量子态验证与基于隐形传态的两回合到一回合归约；同时证明当交换至多3条消息且允许共享纠缠时并行重复不会增大纠缠值。

Result: 给出局部（非纠缠）值严格小于1、但在n≥2重并行重复下等于1的一回合量子博弈与两回合QMIP协议；证明纠缠辅助的零误差经典与量子容量不可被超激活；证明至多3条消息时并行重复的单调性，与经典结果一致。

Conclusion: 量子通信使并行重复产生与经典相反的效果，其根源在于并行重复既增加获胜条件又提供额外量子资源（副本间可联合利用的纠缠）；通过博弈—信道对应，进一步得出纠缠辅助零误差容量不可超激活。

Abstract: We study interactive multiprover games and many-round protocols in which the communication between the verifier and the provers is quantum. Parallel repetition is known to suppress soundness error of classical two-prover games arbitrarily close to zero, as shown by Raz (STOC '95), even with quantum strategies as shown by Yuen (ICALP '16), and Bavarian, Vidick and Yuen (STOC '17). Yet, we show that quantum communication can have the opposite, unexpected effect. Specifically, we exhibit a one-round quantum game and two-round $\mathsf{QMIP}$ protocols whose local (unentangled) value is strictly less than one for a single instance, yet equals one under $n$-fold parallel repetition for every $n \geq 2$. The key is that parallel repetition not only imposes additional winning conditions but also supplies additional quantum resources as entanglement in the exchanged states can be exploited jointly across copies.
  Our multiround examples build on superactivation of zero-error capacities of quantum channels. To establish this connection, we introduce quantum games and interactive protocols which capture the one-shot zero-error classical and quantum capacities, both with and without entanglement assistance. Our constructions use quantum-state verification and a teleportation-based reduction from two rounds to one.
  In contrast, when shared entanglement is allowed, we show that parallel repetition cannot increase the entangled value when at most $3$ messages are exchanged, consistent with classical results of Bellare, Impagliazzo and Naor (FOCS '97), and that of single-prover systems by Kitaev and Watrous (STOC '00). Combining this monotonicity with our quantum game-channel correspondence, we show that entanglement-assisted zero-error classical and quantum capacities cannot be superactivated.

### [2] [Complexity Barriers to State Preparation in Quantum Approximate Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31520)
*Stuart Hadfield*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文刻画了 MaxCut 近似优化（特别是相对随机割的“增益”指标）在量子算法版图中的最坏情况屏障：该屏障在 QRAO 压缩下依然存在，并作用于经典最优值与松弛最优值之间；同时证明纠缠并非障碍来源（乘积态即可达到经典最优），并分离了松弛紧致性、能量近似与操作可达性三者。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近似优化不同于精确优化，其性能度量具有多样性与不同权衡：对 MaxCut 而言，接近 1 的（普通）近似比可以与近乎为零的相对随机割增益并存。标准编码下的无条件经典 MaxCut-Gain 硬度间隙意味着，任何在每一输入上都能以至少逆多项式成功率恢复固定正比例经典最优增益的一致高效量子或混合流程，都会使 NP ⊆ BQP，因而在标准假设下被认为不可能。作者希望在量子算法（含 QRAO）的广阔范围内厘清这一最坏情况屏障何时成立、何时不成立。

Method: 以 MaxCut-Gain 硬度为基准，将障碍分析推广到 QRAO（量子随机访问优化）压缩情形与经典最优—松弛最优区间；利用乘积态在每个输入上可达经典最优这一事实排除纠缠作为障碍来源；针对每量子比特 d∈{2,3} 个变量，采用已知解码器建立编码能量增益到解码均值增益的精确因子为 1/d² 的恒等式；结合该恒等式与 MaxCut-Gain 硬度构造 QRAO 的操作性制备障碍；并构造难点 n 量子比特族，其相对量子松弛超额为 Θ(1/n)。

Result: 屏障在 QRAO 压缩下仍然存活，且作用于经典最优值与松弛最优值之间；对每个输入，乘积态均可达到经典最优，说明逼近经典阈值的障碍不源于对纠缠的需求。对 d∈{2,3}，解码器将编码能量增益按精确因子 1/d² 转为解码均值增益，由此得到 QRAO 的操作性制备障碍。另构造出相对量子松弛超额为 Θ(1/n) 的难点 n 量子比特族；而最大混合态的近似比为 1−Θ(1/n)，其编码能量增益为零，故解码均值增益也为零。

Conclusion: 结果表明，松弛紧致性、能量近似与操作可达性是相互分离的效果：高近似比或高松弛紧致性并不保证可操作意义上的增益。因此在量子及混合优化算法的基准测试与性能评估中，需要更全面地计入这类操作性指标，而不能仅依赖近似比。

Abstract: For many important optimization problems we are restricted to approximate solutions in practice due to computational complexity. Distinct from the exact optimization setting, approximate optimization admits performance measures beyond whether the optimum is found, with different tradeoffs and complexity. For MaxCut, a near-unity (ordinary) approximation ratio can coexist with near-zero improvement (gain) over a random cut. For the standard encoding, the unconditional classical MaxCut-Gain hardness gap implies that \emph{any uniformly efficient quantum or hybrid procedure recovering a fixed positive fraction of the optimal classical gain on every input, with at least inverse-polynomial success probability, would place} NP \emph{in} BQP. Such a procedure is therefore believed impossible under standard assumptions. We broadly address where our worst-case barriers do or do not apply across the quantum algorithm landscape. We prove that the barrier survives quantum random access optimization (QRAO) compression and applies between the classical and relaxed optimal values. For every input, a product state attains the classical optimum. Thus the barrier to reaching the classical threshold does not arise from a need for entanglement. For $d\in\{2,3\}$ variables per qubit, the known decoder transfers encoded energy gain to decoded mean gain by the exact factor $1/d^2$. Combining this identity with MaxCut-Gain hardness gives an operational preparation barrier for QRAO. We also construct hard $n$-qubit families with relative quantum relaxation excess $Θ(1/n)$, while the maximally mixed state has energy approximation ratio $1-Θ(1/n)$, zero encoded energy gain, and hence zero decoded mean gain. Our results separate the effects of relaxation tightness and energy approximation from operational accessibility, motivating more comprehensive accounting in benchmarking and performance assessment.
